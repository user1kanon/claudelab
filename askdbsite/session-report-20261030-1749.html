The two runaway prompts ("check now" at 38.2% and the /goal run at 22.2%) were autonomous loops hitting the stop-hook block cap. Set a tighter exit condition, or raise CLAUDE_CODE_STOP_HOOK_BLOCK_CAP deliberately, instead of re-prompting "check now" / "ignore and continue".
